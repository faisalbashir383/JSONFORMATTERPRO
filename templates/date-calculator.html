❓ FAQ

How are days calculated?+
Days are calculated by finding the absolute difference between two dates in milliseconds, then converting to days. This accounts for leap years and different month lengths.
Does this include the end date?+
The calculation shows the number of days between the two dates. If you want to include both start and end dates, add 1 to the result.
Can I calculate business days only?+
Currently, this calculator counts all calendar days. For business days (excluding weekends), you would need to subtract the weekends manually or use a specialized business day calculator.
