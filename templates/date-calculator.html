Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate days between two dates? +
Simply enter the Start Date and End Date in the inputs above and click "Calculate Duration". The tool will show you the total days, as well as the breakdown in years, months, and weeks.
does this calculator include the end date? +
By default, the calculation represents the duration between the dates. For example, from Jan 1 to Jan 2 is 1 day. If you want to include both the start and end days (inclusive count), simply add 1 to the result.
How are leap years handled? +
Yes, our calculator fully supports leap years. If your date range includes February 29th of a leap year (like 2024 or 2028), it will be counted as an extra day automatically.
Can I subtract days from a date? +
Absolutely. In the "Add/Subtract Days" section, you can enter a negative number (e.g., -30) to find what the date was 30 days ago.
Is this tool free to use for commercial projects? +
Yes, JSON Formatters Pro tools are 100% free for both personal and commercial use. The calculations are performed locally in your browser, ensuring privacy.
