📚 Understanding Calories

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate): Calories your body burns at rest to maintain basic functions like breathing and circulation.

TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure): BMR × activity factor. This is how many calories you burn in a day.

Calorie Deficit/Surplus: To lose weight, eat fewer calories than TDEE. To gain weight, eat more. A 500 calorie daily deficit ≈ 0.5 kg lost per week.
