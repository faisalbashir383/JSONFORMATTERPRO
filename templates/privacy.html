Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters to Us

Last Updated: January 31, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to JSON Formatters Pro. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services.

JSON Formatters Pro is operated by an independent publisher and is not affiliated with Google.

Key Point: JSON Formatters Pro processes all JSON data client-side in your browser. Your JSON data never leaves your device and is not sent to our servers.

Information We Collect

1. Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information about your device, including:

  • Browser Information: Browser type, version, and language preferences
  • Device Information: Operating system, device type, and screen resolution
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, and navigation patterns
  • Cookies: Small text files stored on your device for functionality and analytics

2. Information You Provide

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Contact us through our contact form (name, email, message)
  • Submit feedback or bug reports

3. JSON Data Processing

Important: All JSON formatting, validation, and conversion operations are performed entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. We do not collect, store, or transmit your JSON data to our servers. Your data remains private and secure on your device.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: To provide and maintain our JSON formatting tools
  • Website Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve user experience
  • Communication: To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with our website (via Google Analytics)
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraudulent activity and abuse
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

3. Advertising & Monetization

We may display advertisements through Google AdSense or similar network providers to help keep our tools free. If active, these partners might use cookies or unique device identifiers to deliver relevant ads and track performance. Detailed information on Google's use of data can be found in their privacy and terms.

You can always control your ad preferences or opt out of personalized targeting by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. External Platform Collaborations

We work with various providers for analytics, site hosting, and content delivery networks (CDNs). These platforms process minimal technical details, such as IP addresses or browser versions, to ensure our website stays fast, reliable, and secure.

5. Information Storage & Lifecycle

We store communication records and usage statistics only for the period necessary to improve our service or meet legal mandates. Once the data no longer serves a legitimate business purpose or legal requirement, it is securely removed or anonymized.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Client-Side Processing: JSON data is processed locally in your browser
  • HTTPS Encryption: All data transmission is encrypted using SSL/TLS
  • No Data Storage: We do not store your JSON data on our servers
  • Periodic security reviews and best-practice assessments

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
  • Cookie Control: Manage cookie preferences through browser settings

Children's Privacy

JSON Formatters Pro is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
