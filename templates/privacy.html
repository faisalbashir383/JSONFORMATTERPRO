Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters to Us

Last Updated: March 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how jsonformatterspro.com (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use our website and tools.

1. Data processing

Most tools on this website run client-side in your browser. That means your JSON/text input is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers by default.

2. Information we may collect

  • Usage data: basic analytics such as pages visited, device/browser type, and approximate location (city/country) via analytics providers (if enabled).
  • Cookies: small files used for preferences (like theme) and analytics/ads (if enabled).
  • Support messages: if you contact us via chat/email, we receive the information you share.

3. Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may display ads through Google AdSense or similar providers. These providers may use cookies and/or device identifiers to show relevant ads and to measure ad performance.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services in Google’s privacy documentation.

4. Third-party services

We may use third-party services such as analytics, hosting/CDN, and support chat to operate and improve the website. These services may process limited technical data as described above.

5. Data retention

We retain analytics and support messages only as long as necessary for legitimate business purposes and to comply with legal obligations.

6. Your choices

  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings.
  • You can avoid sharing sensitive data in the tools and when contacting support.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@jsonformatterspro.com.
