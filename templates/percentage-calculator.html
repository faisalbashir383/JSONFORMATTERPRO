📚 How to Calculate Percentages

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The word "percent" comes from the Latin "per centum" meaning "by the hundred." Understanding percentages is essential for everyday tasks like calculating discounts, tips, tax, grades, and financial calculations.

Common Percentage Formulas

X% of Y = Y × (X ÷ 100)
What % is X of Y = (X ÷ Y) × 100
Percentage Change = ((New - Old) ÷ Old) × 100

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number? +
To calculate X% of Y, multiply Y by X and divide by 100. For example, to find 20% of 150: 150 × 20 ÷ 100 = 30. This means 20% of 150 is 30.
How do I calculate percentage increase between two numbers? +
Use the formula: ((New Value - Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100. For example, if a price increases from $50 to $65: ((65 - 50) ÷ 50) × 100 = 30%. The price increased by 30%.
What is the difference between percentage change and percentage points? +
Percentage points measure the absolute difference between two percentages, while percentage change measures the relative change. For example, if interest rates go from 5% to 7%, that's a 2 percentage point increase, but a 40% percentage increase ((7-5)/5 × 100).
How do I calculate the original price before a discount? +
To find the original price before a X% discount, divide the sale price by (1 - X/100). For example, if an item costs $80 after a 20% discount: $80 ÷ (1 - 0.20) = $80 ÷ 0.80 = $100 original price.
How do I convert a fraction to a percentage? +
To convert a fraction to a percentage, divide the numerator by the denominator and multiply by 100. For example, 3/4 = 0.75 × 100 = 75%. Similarly, 1/5 = 0.20 × 100 = 20%.
How do I calculate percentage for grades? +
Divide the points earned by the total possible points and multiply by 100. For example, if you scored 85 out of 100 points: (85 ÷ 100) × 100 = 85%. This gives you your grade percentage.
What does "percent off" mean? +
"Percent off" means a reduction from the original price. For example, "25% off" means you pay 75% of the original price. If an item is $100 with 25% off, you pay $100 × 0.75 = $75.
How do I calculate tip percentage? +
Multiply the bill amount by the tip percentage and divide by 100. For a 15% tip on a $50 bill: $50 × 15 ÷ 100 = $7.50 tip. The total with tip would be $57.50.
How do I calculate compound percentage? +
For compound percentage growth, use the formula: Final = Initial × (1 + rate)^periods. For example, $1000 growing at 5% for 3 years: $1000 × (1.05)³ = $1157.63. This accounts for interest on interest.
