💡 About Cron Expressions

Cron is a time-based job scheduler in Unix-like operating systems. A cron expression consists of 5 fields that define when a job should run: Minute (0-59), Hour (0-23), Day of Month (1-31), Month (1-12), and Day of Week (0-6, where 0 is Sunday).

Special characters: * (any), */n (every n), - (range), , (list)
