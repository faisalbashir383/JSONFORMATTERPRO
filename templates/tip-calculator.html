❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I tip? +
Standard restaurant tip is 15-20%. For excellent service, 20-25% is appropriate. Some countries have different customs.
Should I tip on tax? +
Traditionally, tip is calculated on the pre-tax bill (subtotal). However, many people tip on the total including tax for simplicity.
When is it okay not to tip? +
Tipping is optional when service is included (check your bill), at fast-food restaurants, or for counter service. In some countries like Japan, tipping is not customary.
