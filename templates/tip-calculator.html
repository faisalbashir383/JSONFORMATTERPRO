📚 Tipping Guide

Restaurants: 15-20% for good service, 20-25% for excellent service.

Delivery: 15-20% or minimum $3-5 for food delivery.

Hair/Spa: 15-20% of the service cost.

Hotels: $1-5 per bag for bellhops, $2-5 daily for housekeeping.

❓ FAQ

How much should I tip?+
Standard restaurant tip is 15-20%. For excellent service, 20-25% is appropriate. Some countries have different customs.
Should I tip on tax?+
Traditionally, tip is calculated on the pre-tax bill. However, many people tip on the total including tax for simplicity.
When is it okay not to tip?+
Tipping is optional when service is included (check your bill), at fast-food restaurants, or for counter service. In some countries, tipping isn't customary.
