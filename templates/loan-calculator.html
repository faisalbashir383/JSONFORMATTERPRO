📚 Understanding EMI

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is a fixed monthly payment made to repay a loan. Each EMI consists of two parts: principal repayment and interest payment. In the early months, a larger portion goes toward interest; later, more goes toward principal.

EMI Formula

EMI = [P × r × (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]

Where: P = Principal, r = Monthly interest rate (annual/12/100), n = Number of months

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI?+
EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It's a fixed amount you pay every month to repay your loan over a specified period. Each EMI includes both principal and interest components.
How is EMI calculated?+
EMI is calculated using the reducing balance method formula that considers your loan amount, interest rate, and tenure. The formula ensures equal monthly payments throughout the loan period.
Can I reduce my EMI?+
Yes! You can reduce EMI by choosing a longer tenure, negotiating a lower interest rate, or making a larger down payment. However, longer tenure means more total interest paid.
What happens if I prepay my loan?+
Prepayment reduces your principal, which decreases total interest and can shorten your tenure or reduce EMI. Some lenders charge prepayment penalties, so check your loan terms.
