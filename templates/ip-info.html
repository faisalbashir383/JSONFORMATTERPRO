💡 About IP Address Lookup

An IP (Internet Protocol) address is a unique identifier assigned to every device connected to the internet. This tool allows you to look up any IP address and get detailed geolocation information including country, city, timezone, ISP, and approximate coordinates.

What you can find: Country and region, city and ZIP code, timezone, ISP (Internet Service Provider), organization name, AS number, and geographic coordinates.

Use cases: Security analysis, fraud detection, geo-targeting, troubleshooting network issues, understanding user location, and verifying VPN connections.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is an IP address?

An IP (Internet Protocol) address is a unique identifier assigned to every device connected to the internet. IPv4 uses 32-bit addresses (e.g., 8.8.8.8), while IPv6 uses 128-bit.

How accurate is IP geolocation?

IP geolocation is typically accurate to the city level (about 80% accuracy). It can identify country with 99% accuracy but may not pinpoint exact street addresses.

What is my public IP address?

Your public IP is the address assigned by your ISP that identifies you on the internet. Use our 'My IP' button to instantly discover your current public IP.

What is an ISP?

ISP (Internet Service Provider) is the company that provides your internet connection. Examples include Comcast, AT&T, Verizon, and regional providers.

Can IP addresses reveal my exact location?

No. IP addresses typically reveal city-level location, not exact addresses. For precise location, websites need GPS or explicit permission from your device.

🏠 What Is My IP Address?

Your public IP address is the unique identifier assigned to your device by your Internet Service Provider (ISP). This is the address that websites and online services see when you connect to the internet. Unlike your private (local) IP address which is used within your home network, your public IP is visible to the outside world.

To find your current public IP address, simply click the "My IP" button above. The IP address lookup tool will instantly show your IP along with your approximate location, ISP name, and other geolocation details. This is useful for troubleshooting network issues, verifying VPN connections, or checking what information websites can see about your connection.
