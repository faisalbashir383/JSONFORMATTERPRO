💡 About IP Address Lookup

An IP (Internet Protocol) address is a unique identifier assigned to every device connected to the internet. This tool allows you to look up any IP address and get detailed geolocation information including country, city, timezone, ISP, and approximate coordinates.

What you can find: Country and region, city and ZIP code, timezone, ISP (Internet Service Provider), organization name, AS number, and geographic coordinates.

Use cases: Security analysis, fraud detection, geo-targeting, troubleshooting network issues, understanding user location, and verifying VPN connections.
