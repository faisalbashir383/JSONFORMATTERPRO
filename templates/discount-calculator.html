How to Calculate Discount

Calculating a discount is simple. Here is the step-by-step formula:

  • Step 1: Convert the percentage to a decimal (e.g., 20% = 0.20).
  • Step 2: Multiply the original price by the decimal (100 × 0.20 = 20).
  • Step 3: Subtract the result from the original price (100 - 20 = 80).

Formula: Price - (Price × Discount%) = Sale Price

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 20% off? +
To calculate 20% off, simply multiply the original price by 0.20. That is your savings amount. Subtract that from the original price to get the final cost. For example, 20% off $50 is $10 savings, making the final price $40.
Can I calculate double discounts? +
Yes, but do it sequentially. If you have "20% off plus an extra 10% off", first calculate the price with 20% off. Then take 10% off that NEW price. It is NOT the same as 30% off!
What is the formula for sale price? +
The formula is: Sale Price = Original Price - (Original Price × Discount Rate).
