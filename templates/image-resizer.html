❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image resizer free?

Yes, completely free with no limits. No signup or registration required. Use it as many times as you need.

Is my image data secure?

Absolutely! All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript. Your images are never uploaded to any server - they stay on your device.

What image formats are supported?

We support JPG, PNG, WebP, and GIF formats for input. You can output in JPG, PNG, or WebP format with adjustable quality.

Can I resize to multiple sizes at once?

Yes! Select multiple social media presets or enter custom dimensions. All resized images can be downloaded individually or as a ZIP file.

How does the AI background remover work?

Our AI background remover uses a machine learning neural network that runs entirely in your browser. It automatically detects the subject and removes the background, creating a transparent PNG.

Why does background removal take time on first use?

The first time you use background removal, the AI model (~40MB) is downloaded to your browser. This is cached, so subsequent uses are much faster.

What types of images work best with AI background removal?

The AI works best with photos of people, products, animals, and objects with clear edges against distinct backgrounds. Very complex or camouflaged subjects may be challenging.

Can I resize and remove background together?

Yes! First remove the background to get a transparent image, then resize it to your desired dimensions. The transparency is preserved when you output as PNG.
