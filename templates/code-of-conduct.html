🤝 Code of Conduct

Building a welcoming and inclusive community for all developers

🌟 Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

📋 Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

✅ Be Respectful

Treat everyone with respect and consideration. Value different viewpoints and experiences.

✅ Be Inclusive

Use welcoming and inclusive language. Be mindful of your words and their impact on others.

✅ Be Constructive

Provide helpful feedback. Focus on what is best for the community and learning together.

✅ Be Professional

Maintain professionalism in all interactions. Keep discussions focused and productive.

🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

  • Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind
  • Offensive comments related to personal characteristics
  • Trolling, insulting remarks, or personal attacks
  • Public or private harassment
  • Publishing others' private information without permission
  • Sexual language or imagery in any context
  • Threats of violence or violent language
  • Spam, excessive self-promotion, or off-topic content
  • Other conduct inappropriate for a professional setting

🔧 For Tool Usage

When using our developer tools:

  • Do not attempt to exploit vulnerabilities or attack our systems
  • Do not use our tools to process illegal or harmful content
  • Do not abuse the tools in ways that impact other users
  • Report any bugs or security issues responsibly

💬 For Comments & Interactions

When engaging with blog posts or contacting us:

  • Stay on topic and contribute meaningfully
  • Be patient with those learning new concepts
  • Share knowledge generously with others
  • Credit others when using their ideas or code

⚖️ Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

  • Warning: Private notice explaining the violation
  • Temporary Ban: Suspension from community interactions
  • Permanent Ban: Permanent exclusion from community spaces

📝 Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through our Contact Page. All reports will be reviewed and investigated confidentially.

🙏 Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.0.

💚 Thank You

Thank you for helping make JSON Formatters Pro a welcoming place for all developers!
