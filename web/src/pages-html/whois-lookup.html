What Is WHOIS?

WHOIS (pronounced "who is") is a publicly accessible database that contains registration information for domain names. When someone registers a domain, their contact information and registration details are stored in WHOIS records maintained by domain registrars and registry operators. This information helps identify who owns a domain and how to contact them.

WHOIS data typically includes the domain registrant's name and organization, contact email addresses, the registrar through which the domain was registered, registration and expiration dates, nameserver information, and domain status codes. While privacy protection services can mask personal details, the technical registration information remains accessible for most domains.
