📚 The Power of Compound Interest

Compound interest is interest calculated on both the initial principal and the accumulated interest. This creates exponential growth over time, often called the "eighth wonder of the world."

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) + PMT × ((1 + r/n)^(nt) - 1) / (r/n)

Rule of 72: Divide 72 by your interest rate to estimate how many years it takes to double your money. At 8% interest, money doubles in about 9 years.
