Frequently Asked Questions

Is this resume builder really free?
Yes, 100% free! No hidden fees, no premium plans, no watermarks. You can create unlimited resumes and download them as PDF without any cost.
Do I need to create an account?
No! You can start building your resume immediately without signing up. Your data is saved locally in your browser.
Is my data secure and private?
Absolutely! All your data stays in your browser - we never upload or store your personal information on any server. Your resume is 100% private.
What is an ATS-friendly resume?
ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software used by employers to scan resumes. Our templates use clean formatting, standard fonts, and proper structure that ATS systems can easily read and parse.
Can I save my resume and edit it later?
Yes! Your resume auto-saves every 5 seconds to your browser. You can also export it as JSON and import it anytime to continue editing.
What file formats can I download?
You can download your resume as a high-quality PDF file, perfect for job applications. You can also export your data as JSON for backup.
How do I switch between templates?
Simply click on the template buttons (Modern, Professional, Developer, Creative) at the top of the preview panel. Your content stays the same - only the design changes!
Can I add multiple work experiences?
Yes! Click "Add Experience" to add as many work experiences as you need. You can also add multiple education entries, projects, and certifications.
How do I add skills?
Type a skill in the input field and press Enter to add it as a tag. You can add both technical and soft skills separately.
What should I include in my resume?
Focus on: Contact info, a strong summary, relevant work experience with quantified achievements, education, key skills, and notable projects. Tailor your content to the job you're applying for.
